Surface wear maps in this folder are built from these ambientCG materials, released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain): https://ambientcg.com/ and
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- grain.png: SurfaceImperfections020 (normal, roughness, opacity), by ambientCG.
- scratches.png: Scratches002 (normal, opacity) and Scratches004 (opacity), by ambientCG.

The maps were repacked by channel for PolyLoupe: grain = normal X, normal Y, roughness, dust;
scratches = normal X, normal Y, scratch mask, brushed mask.
