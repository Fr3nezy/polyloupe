# PolyLoupe name and logo

The code of PolyLoupe is free software under the GNU GPL (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)): you can use,
study, change and share it, including for money, as the license allows.

The name "PolyLoupe" and the PolyLoupe logo are not covered by that license. They identify the
builds published by the project, so people know what they are getting.

You may:

- use the name to talk about PolyLoupe, link to it, review it or write tutorials;
- keep the name and logo in unmodified builds you redistribute from the official sources, and say
  that your package "contains PolyLoupe";
- say that your own project is "based on PolyLoupe" or "a fork of PolyLoupe".

Please don't, without written permission:

- publish a modified version under the name "PolyLoupe" or with the logo; rename your fork and
  replace the logo (the files in `assets/brand`);
- sell PolyLoupe, or anything with its name or logo, in a way that suggests it is the official
  build or that the project endorses you;
- use the name or logo in your product, company or domain name.

Questions and permission requests: open an issue on the project's repository.
